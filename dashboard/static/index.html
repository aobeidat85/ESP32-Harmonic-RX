Connection help

Keep the ESP32 receiver connected to this PC and the experimental GPIO sender unplugged. Place the receiver near the outdoor sensor. If the serial port is unavailable, close other serial monitors or reconnect the receiver, then select Start receiving.

Readings persist when you stop reception. CSV exports include their source and packet checks.
